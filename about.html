About Girls Unplugged

A sisterhood created so girls don't have to grow alone.

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle is a girl-led online sisterhood where girls can connect, express themselves, have honest conversations, discover opportunities, laugh, learn and grow together.

How it started

It began with a simple “what if?”

What if girls had a space where they could simply be girls — without having to pretend they had everything figured out?

Girls Unplugged started from the desire to create a warm, honest and intentional space where girls could talk about real life, ask questions, make friendships, laugh about random things, celebrate their wins and support one another through difficult moments.

It was not built from a huge office or a complicated system. It started with what was available, with a willingness to create something meaningful and with the belief that community can make growing up feel a little less lonely.

Today, the Bloom & Glow Circle is growing into a wider sisterhood of girls from different backgrounds and places — connected by the simple idea that we can grow better together.

Why we exist

Because girls deserve community, not comparison.

We want Girls Unplugged to be a place where belonging comes before performance and curiosity is welcomed instead of judged.

Belonging

A space where girls can feel welcomed, valued and reminded that they are not alone.

Honest conversations

Room for real questions, thoughtful discussions and conversations that meet girls where they are.

Growth

Encouragement to learn, reflect, try again, discover new possibilities and keep becoming.

Joy

Because sisterhood should include laughter, games, little celebrations and genuinely fun moments.

Respect

We make room for different experiences, backgrounds, opinions and journeys.

Voice

Girls should be able to express themselves, ask questions and contribute to the spaces they belong to.

What we're building

A digital home for girlhood.

Girls Unplugged is growing beyond a group chat. We are building an ecosystem of conversations, community experiences, resources, opportunities and connections that girls can return to at different stages of their journeys.

Connection — friendships and meaningful community across borders.
Confidence — spaces that encourage girls to use their voices.
Learning — conversations, resources and opportunities to keep discovering.
Possibility — reminders that a girl's current circumstances do not have to define her future.

The Girls Unplugged promise

Come as you are.
Leave a little more you.

You do not need to be the loudest person in the room. You do not need to have your life perfectly planned. You do not need to know exactly who you are becoming yet.

There is space here to learn, change your mind, ask questions, make mistakes, celebrate small wins and grow at your own pace.
