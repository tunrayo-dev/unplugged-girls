About Girls Unplugged

A space to connect, grow and simply be.

Girls Unplugged is a growing online sisterhood created by girls, for girls — built around connection, confidence, wellbeing, learning and the simple reminder that no girl has to figure everything out alone.

Why Girls Unplugged exists

Sometimes, girls just need a place to breathe.

Growing up can come with questions, pressure, uncertainty, excitement and moments when you simply want someone who understands.

Girls Unplugged was created to make room for those moments. It is an online community where girls can meet other girls, talk honestly, learn from one another, have fun and grow without feeling like they have to perform.

At the heart of it is the Bloom & Glow Circle — our community space where girls from different places can show up, participate and build genuine connections.

Our story

Created by girls, for girls.

Girls Unplugged began with a simple idea: girls deserve spaces where they can feel seen, heard and connected.

01

A sisterhood first

The community is designed around friendship and belonging. Girls can meet one another across borders and discover that their experiences may be different, but they do not have to feel alone.

02

Room to grow

Growth does not always look like a major achievement. Sometimes it is finding your voice, learning something new, taking care of yourself or having the courage to try again.

03

A community that keeps moving

Since launching on September 29, 2025, Girls Unplugged has continued to grow through weekly activities, conversations, guest speaker sessions and community experiences.

What we believe

The kind of community we're building.

Belonging matters.

Every girl should have spaces where she feels welcome, respected and able to participate.

Connection can change things.

Meeting people who understand parts of your experience can make difficult seasons feel a little less lonely.

Growth looks different for everyone.

There is no single timeline for confidence, learning, healing, discovery or finding your voice.

Girls should have a voice.

Girls deserve opportunities to contribute ideas, lead, ask questions, create and shape the spaces they belong to.
