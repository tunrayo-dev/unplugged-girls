Bloom & Glow Circle

Your people are out there.

The Bloom & Glow Circle is the community at the heart of Girls Unplugged — an online sisterhood where girls can connect, talk, learn, laugh and grow together.

Join the Sisterhood See how it works
More than a group chat

A space where girls can actually be themselves.

The Circle is built around connection. It is a place where girls can meet others from different backgrounds and countries, participate in conversations and activities, and build friendships along the way.

There is no pressure to have everything figured out. You can come into the community curious, quiet, excited, unsure, talkative or somewhere in between.

The goal is simple: create a space where girls feel comfortable showing up as themselves and know there are other girls growing alongside them.

How the Circle works

There is always something to come back to.

Our community rhythm gives Bloomies regular opportunities to connect without making participation feel like another obligation.

Monday

Motivation Monday

A moment to reset, encourage one another and step into the week with intention.

Wednesday

Wellness Wednesday

A space to check in, talk about wellbeing and make room for healthier ways of showing up for ourselves.

Friday

Fun Friday

A lighter space for games, laughter, conversations and simply enjoying being together.
