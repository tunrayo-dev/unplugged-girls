Girls Unplugged is an online sisterhood created by girls, for girls. It gives girls a space to connect, talk, learn, have fun and grow together.

The Bloom & Glow Circle is the community at the heart of Girls Unplugged. It is the online sisterhood where Bloomies connect and take part in community activities.

Girls Unplugged is built around creating a supportive online community for girls. The exact community participation arrangements may vary as the Circle grows, so prospective members can contact the team for current details.

Bloomies currently come from Eswatini, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Kenya, Liberia, The Gambia and Mozambique.

Community activities include Motivation Monday, Wellness Wednesday and Fun Friday. The community also has guest speaker sessions, quarterly community calls and other special activities.

The Circle has weekly activities through Motivation Monday, Wellness Wednesday and Fun Friday. Community calls are held quarterly, alongside other special activities and guest speaker sessions.

The Opportunity Centre is a Girls Unplugged group where weekly opportunities can be shared with girls in the community. These may include scholarships, fellowships, competitions, internships, exchanges and other youth opportunities.

Opportunities can be shared through the Opportunity Centre. Because eligibility requirements and deadlines can change, girls should always verify an opportunity on the original organisation's website before applying.
