Bloom & Glow Circle

Your place in the sisterhood starts here.

The Bloom & Glow Circle is an online sisterhood created by girls, for girls.

It is a space to connect, talk, learn, have fun and grow alongside other girls. The community currently includes Bloomies from 10 countries and continues to grow.

Learn About the Circle
Opportunity Centre

Keep an eye out for what is next.

Girls Unplugged has an Opportunity Centre group where weekly opportunities can be shared with the community.

Weekly opportunity sharing

Opportunities should be easier to find.

The Opportunity Centre is intended to help Bloomies discover opportunities they might otherwise miss, including scholarships, fellowships, competitions, internships, exchanges and other youth-focused opportunities.

Requirements and deadlines can change, so every opportunity should be checked against its original source before applying.
