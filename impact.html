What impact looks like

Four areas sit at the heart of our community.

These are the outcomes Girls Unplugged is intentionally creating space for through the Bloom & Glow Circle.

01

Connection

Girls have a place to meet, talk, listen and build meaningful friendships across borders.

02

Personal Growth

The Circle creates room for reflection, confidence, wellbeing and learning through everyday community experiences.

03

Voice & Leadership

Girls are encouraged to participate, contribute ideas, take initiative and discover their own voice.

04

Possibility

Through conversations, resources and shared experiences, girls can discover possibilities beyond what they already know.

Beyond one location

A digital community can cross borders.

Girls Unplugged brings together Bloomies from different countries and backgrounds through an online community.

The value of that connection is not simply geographic. It gives girls opportunities to encounter experiences, perspectives and friendships they might not otherwise have access to.

As the community grows, so does the range of stories and perspectives represented inside it.

Growing with the community

Impact is something we continue to learn about.

As Girls Unplugged grows, we want to keep listening to the girls who make the community what it is.

Listen to Bloomies

The community should continue to respond to what girls actually need, value and enjoy.

Keep the space human

Growth should never come at the expense of the connection and care that made the community meaningful in the first place.

Create useful experiences

Activities and resources should have a reason for being there and add something useful to girls' experiences.

Grow responsibly

A growing community requires thoughtful leadership, clear communication and transparency about what exists.
