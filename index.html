More than a group chat

A place to be seen, heard and supported.

Growing up can feel exciting, confusing, loud and lonely — sometimes all at once. Girls' Unplugged exists to make sure girls have a place where they can show up honestly, meet other girls, learn from one another and remember that they are not alone.

We create room for conversations, friendships, confidence-building, reflection, laughter and opportunities. No pressure to have everything figured out. Just a community where growth is allowed to look different for everyone.

Our weekly rhythm

Connect. Reflect. Laugh. Learn. Bloom.

Different days, different energy — but one purpose: helping girls feel connected and supported.

Monday

Motivation Monday

Start the week with reminders, encouragement and little pushes to keep going.

Wednesday

Wellness Wednesday

Honest conversations and gentle check-ins around wellbeing, emotions and self-care.

Friday

Fun Friday

Games, laughs, anonymous moments and the kind of fun that brings everyone closer.

Saturday

Reflection Saturday

A slower space to pause, look back on the week and think about what comes next.

The Bloom & Glow Circle

Because girls deserve community, not comparison.

Our Circle brings girls from different places and backgrounds into one intentional space. We talk about real life, celebrate wins, ask questions, share experiences and make room for each other.

Learn from real voices

Conversations that leave you with something.

Every first Friday of the month, we create space for a guest speaker to share experiences, lessons and perspectives with the Circle. We also host special conversations when there is something worth bringing to the table.

The girl behind the circle

Built from a simple belief: girls should never feel like they have to figure everything out alone.

Girls' Unplugged was founded by Motunrayo Linda Idowu, a youth leader, technologist, speaker and girls' empowerment advocate who wanted to create the kind of sisterhood she knew girls could benefit from.
