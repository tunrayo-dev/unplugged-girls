Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

A simple explanation of how Girls Unplugged approaches information shared through this website.

On this page
Information we receive How information is used Information sharing Third-party services Security Children and young people Policy changes Contact

Last updated: September 2026

Information we receive

Girls Unplugged may receive information that you voluntarily provide when you contact the team by email, suggest a resource, enquire about speaking, explore a collaboration or otherwise communicate with us.

The type of information shared depends on the reason for contacting us. Please avoid sending passwords, financial information or other highly sensitive personal information through ordinary email.

How information is used

Information you choose to share may be used to respond to your message, understand your request, communicate about a potential collaboration or manage a relevant Girls Unplugged activity.

We aim to use information only for the purpose for which it was reasonably provided and for related communication where appropriate.

Information sharing

Girls Unplugged does not intend to sell personal information submitted through this website.

Information may be shared when necessary to respond to a request, operate a relevant service, comply with a legal obligation or protect the safety and rights of people involved with the community.

Third-party services

This website may link to external websites and platforms. These services have their own privacy practices and policies, which are outside the control of Girls Unplugged.

When you follow an external link, review the privacy information of that service before providing personal information.

Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessarily exposing information shared with us. However, no online communication or internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For your own protection, do not send passwords, financial details or other sensitive credentials through the contact email.

Children and young people

Girls Unplugged is a girl-centred community, and we recognise that some people who interact with our platforms may be under the age of 18.

We encourage young people to protect their personal information online and to avoid publicly sharing details such as home addresses, passwords, private contact information or other information that could put them at risk.

Where participation in a particular activity requires additional consent or safeguarding arrangements, those requirements should be followed before participation.

Policy changes

This privacy policy may be updated as Girls Unplugged, its website and its community practices develop.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have a question about this privacy policy or about information you have shared with Girls Unplugged, you can contact the team by email.

grlsunplugged@gmail.com

Questions?

We are here to help.

If something about this policy is unclear, you can reach out to the Girls Unplugged team.
