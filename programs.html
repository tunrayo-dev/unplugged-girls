Our approach

Not every meaningful moment has to be a big event.

Girls Unplugged focuses on creating consistent opportunities for connection rather than filling the calendar for the sake of having more activities.

01

Consistency

Regular touchpoints help the community feel like a space girls can return to.

02

Participation

Activities create room for girls to contribute, respond, share and take part in their own way.

03

Variety

Community should make space for reflection, wellbeing, learning, laughter and connection.

04

Belonging

The purpose behind every activity is ultimately the same: helping girls feel connected to something bigger than themselves.
