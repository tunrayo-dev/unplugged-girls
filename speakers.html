Voices in the Circle

Different stories. Different perspectives. Shared space.

Our guest speaker sessions give Bloomies opportunities to listen, ask questions and learn from people invited into the Girls Unplugged community.

Why speakers matter

Sometimes one conversation can open a new possibility.

Guest speaker sessions are one of the ways Girls Unplugged brings new voices and experiences into the Bloom & Glow Circle.

They create space for Bloomies to listen, ask questions, reflect on what they hear and take away ideas that may become useful in their own journeys.

We are grateful to every speaker who has made time to contribute to the community.
