The Girls Unplugged Team

A community built by people who care.

Behind the Bloom & Glow Circle is a small team helping create the conversations, activities, connections and experiences that keep the community moving.

Behind the Circle

Leadership here is about showing up.

Girls Unplugged is intentionally shaped by people who understand that community is built through consistency, care and the willingness to do the work behind the scenes.

Our team members contribute across community engagement, programming, communications, content and creative work.

How we work

Titles matter less than what you do with them.

Our team is built around the same values we want the community to experience.

01

Show up

Community work depends on consistency, even when the work happening behind the scenes is not visible.

02

Listen

Good community leadership starts with paying attention to the people the community exists for.

03

Collaborate

Different strengths and perspectives make the work better when they are brought together.

04

Keep learning

As the community changes, the team has to remain open to learning, adapting and improving.
